## Key findings
- (p. 1) Today the **day-ahead market (one-day horizon) is the most important market for Norwegian producers**. Storable water allows flexibility, for example exporting to Denmark at low wind and importing wind power back at surplus.
- (p. 1) **"Participation in several markets increases the value of Norwegian hydropower significantly"**, and the need for flexibility and system services grows with solar and wind.
- (p. 1–2) The model suite spans long-term system and reservoir management over seasons and years down to operational scheduling and market bidding for single watercourses: **EMPS/EOPS, FanSi, ProdRisk, Samnett/SamLast, SHOP, SHARM, PriMod**.
- (p. 2–3) Related projects: **"Integrating Balancing Markets in Hydropower Scheduling Methods" (2014–2017)**, MultiSHARM (day-ahead bidding with multiple short-term markets, 2015–2018), PriBas (2017–2020), MAD (2015–2018), Rakett (2020–2022).

## Strengths
