                                                                      Upon convergence of the SDDP algorithm, the resulting cuts
                                                                   are obtained. During the execution of the algorithm, cuts gen-
                                                                   erated in each iteration are incorporated as constraints within
                                                                   the SDDP framework. The final set of cuts is subsequently
                                                                   implemented as constraints in the simulation model with a
                                                                   predefined number of iterations to estimate the revenues.

                                                                      After conducting the simulations, the Net Present Value
                                                                   (NPV) method found in Figure 5 is applied to evaluate the
                                                                   incremental cash flows generated by the various capacity
                                                                   expansion projects described in Section III.

                                                                      2) Model assumptions: The proposed methodology relies
                                                                   on several simplifying assumptions. The hydropower plant is
                                                                   assumed to be sufficiently small to operate as a price taker
                                                                   within the relevant price zone, and similar assumptions are
                                                                   commonly adopted in the literature, as observed in Helseth et
                                                                   al. [9]. Capacity markets are assumed to remain unsaturated
                                                                   and modeled without activation, implying that revenues stem
                                                                   solely from capacity procurements. To avoid computational
                                                                   complexity, the model does not consider start/stop conditions.

                                                                      Long-term uncertainty is represented through a weekly
                                                                   Markov chain, which may limit the persistence and severity
                                                                   of extreme price-inflow combinations. Prolonged periods of
                                                                   scarcity or flooding would likely exacerbate price conditions.
