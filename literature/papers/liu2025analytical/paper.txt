HIGHLIGHTS

· Developed two PSH economic dispatch models with non-zero lower bounds.
· Applied dynamic programming to derive optimal multi-period PSH dispatch policies.
· Used auxiliary variables to address discontinuities in reward and value functions.
· Non-zero limits increase idle probability, reducing PSH profitability.
· Validated model with synthetic and real data, demonstrating practical applications.

                           can derive unique, analytically optimal economic dispatch decisions for each period. Our findings indicate that
