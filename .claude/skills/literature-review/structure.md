# Structure of a literature review: chapter and sections

Evidence: 12 reviews, 316 coded paragraphs (`theses/lit-review-skill/codebook/CODING_RESULTS.md`), sizes in `measures/REPORT.md`. Each rule names what it rests on: **all** (the 12), **closest four** (Bore 2026, Bore & Brensholm 2025, EMPIRE 2024, Norland 2025) or **Bore** (the two Bore theses). Where the three disagree, the closest four win, then Bore.

## Chapter
| Element | Evidence | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Own chapter after the background, before the problem description | all four closest; 8 of 12 overall | Own chapter (ours: ch. 5). |
| Opening paragraph(s): what is reviewed, why, in which order | OPEN paragraphs are 12–14 % of all paragraphs; 29–33 % of section-first paragraphs | One or two OPEN paragraphs; no studies in them. |
| Search strategy section | Bore only (both) | Include it: how the earlier review was extended, quality criteria, backward snowballing with a cited method. Past tense. |
| Themes | closest four: 5 level-2 sections of about 450 words; all: 2–3 of about 530 | 4–6 themes, each a part of our own problem, in the order of the problem; solution methods last. |
| Sub-sections | Bore 2026 splits two themes into 3–4 numbered sub-sections of 1–4 paragraphs; the other three do not | Allowed where a theme has distinct sub-questions (uncertainty, markets); otherwise run-in headings. |
| Comparability caveat before numbers | Bore 2026 (4.4, second paragraph); Bore & Brensholm (4.4) | One paragraph saying results from different markets, plants and baselines are not directly comparable, placed before the first effect size. |
| Closing section: gap and contribution | GAP paragraphs 11–12 % of all; 23–25 % of section-last paragraphs; all four closest have a named "contribution" section | A final section: what remains unexplored (named nearest studies and what they did not do), what this thesis does, the research questions, and the comparison table with our study as the last row. |
| Comparison table | all four closest | Columns are the dimensions our thesis varies. |

## A theme section
Median 3 paragraphs and 450 words (closest four), 4 distinct works, 5 citations.

| Position | What stands there (closest four) | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| First paragraph | OPEN 33 %, CLAIM 29 %, GAP 12 %, STUDY 12 % | Open with a claim about the field or the problem (or a short OPEN that says what the section asks). Never open a section with a single study (4 % in Bore). |
| Middle paragraphs | STUDY 37 %, GROUP 27 %, CRITIQUE 10 %, COMPARE 8 %, CLAIM 8 % | The evidence: studies in depth, groups of studies, comparisons. At most two single-study paragraphs in a row. |
| Last paragraph | SYNTH 25 %, GAP 25 %, OPEN(next) 17 %, CLAIM 12 % | Close on what the section's literature establishes or leaves open. In the closest four, half of all sections end on a synthesis or a gap; in the other theses, a quarter. |
