# Nordic aFRR procurement volumes, Q4 2026 (Nordic TSOs, 2026)

**Source file:** `source.pdf` / `source.txt` (copied from the inbox). The inbox marks it as an **attachment, not the cited source**: it is the PDF linked from Statnett's aFRR page (`ph24_statnett_afrr`), original URL `https://www.statnett.no/globalassets/for-aktorer-i-kraftsystemet/marked/reservemarkeder/afrr/market-information-afrr-procurement_q4-2026.pdf` (see `69_Statnett_2025e_KILDEINFO.md`). The hourly table is garbled by `pdftotext -layout`; values below were read with `pdftotext -raw`. Page 1 = table, page 2 = TSO shares.

## Research question / purpose
Announce the hourly aFRR volumes the Nordic TSOs procure from 1 October 2026 and how they are shared between TSOs (p. 1).

## Setting
- **System:** Nordic LFC block (DK2, FI, NO, SE).
- **Markets:** Nordic aFRR capacity market (hourly, separate up/down).
- **Horizon and resolution:** Q4 2026, per hour (CET), all days.

## Method
- n/a. Procurement based on the TSOs' frequency-quality target; frequency quality "well within the goal for 2026" (p. 1).

## Data / case
- (p. 1) Downward aFRR in **hours 7–19 reduced from 325 MW to 275 MW**; upward unchanged.
- (p. 1, table, CET hours) **Up:** 250 MW in hours 1 and 24 (00–01, 23–24), **200 MW** in hours 2–23. **Down:** 300 MW in hours 1 and 24; 250 MW in hours 2–6 and 20–23; **275 MW in hours 7–19**.
- (p. 2) Shares of the Nordic volume: **Statnett 31 % up / 31 % down**; Svenska kraftnät 39 / 38 %; Fingrid 20 / 20 %; Energinet (DK2) 10 / 11 %.
- Derived (our arithmetic, not in the source): Norway's share ≈ 62–78 MW up and ≈ 78–93 MW down per hour.

## Key findings
- (p. 1) aFRR is procured **for all hours and all days**; volumes differ between up and down and by time of day.
- (p. 1) TSOs evaluate and reassess the procurement scheme continuously (volumes change quarterly).

## Strengths
- Concrete, current volume numbers; shows the size of the market relative to a single plant.

## Weaknesses / limitations
- Valid for Q4 2026 only; earlier/later quarters differ. Nordic aFRR CM clears across bidding zones, so Statnett's share is not a hard Norwegian quota (cross-zonal exchange up to 10–20 % of capacity: `ph24_statnett_bsp_terms_2025`, Art. 14).
