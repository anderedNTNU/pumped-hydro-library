# Norges vannbalanse i TWh (Holmqvist, NVE, 2017)

**Source:** `source.pdf` (42 pages, Norwegian), NVE Rapport 87-2017, https://publikasjoner.nve.no/rapport/2017/rapport2017_87.pdf (KILDEINFO). It came into the inbox as an attachment ("VEDLEGG") to the NVE web page "Hydrologiske data til kraftsituasjonsrapporten" (group label NVE_2024a), which is a separate source (a folder `nve2026hydrologiske` exists for it). The title page lists Erik Holmqvist as editor ("Redaktør"); the author field is blank. Printed page numbers = PDF page numbers.

## Research question / purpose
Recalibrate NVE's 82 HBV models (with new met.no 1 km gridded precipitation/temperature, revised rating curves) and document Norway's water balance in TWh — precipitation energy, usable inflow, snow store, soil/groundwater — and its variation 1958–2016 (pp. 2, 5–8).

## Setting
- **System:** the whole Norwegian hydropower system, split into NVE's 4 Samkjøringsmodell areas: 1 Øst-/Sørlandet, 2 Vestlandet (Lysefjorden–Stadt), 3 Midt-Norge, 4 Nord-Norge (p. 6).
- **Horizon and resolution:** 59 years (1958–2016), weekly series; normal period 1981–2010.

## Method
- **Model type:** HBV rainfall–runoff models for 82 gauging stations; flows converted to energy (GWh) with station-specific factors from the Samkjøringsmodell (~1,100 modules) (p. 6, p. 11).
- **Validation:** HBV weekly usable inflow vs Nord Pool inflow (from production and reservoir changes), r² = 0.96 for 2010–2016 (p. 5, p. 15).
- **Statistics:** trend analysis, inter-area correlations, frequency analysis (normal distribution on annual/seasonal aggregates) (pp. 16–38).

## Key findings
- (p. 30) Annual usable inflow varied by 76 TWh (95–171 TWh); variation is smaller than for precipitation (87 TWh) because snow and soil water smooth it.
- (p. 31) Inflow is strongly correlated between neighbouring regions (~0.8), so a drought in Øst-/Sørlandet can coincide with one in Vestlandet (as in 2010).
- (p. 32) Despite an upward long-run trend, single years can still be as dry as in the 1960s.
- (p. 23) Snow on 1 January explains only part (r² = 0.41) of the year's maximum snow store; by 1 March r² = 0.87: inflow uncertainty resolves gradually through the winter.

## Strengths
- Authoritative long-series hydrology statistics for the Norwegian system; validated against Nord Pool data.

## Weaknesses / limitations
- National/regional aggregates in TWh, not plant-level m³/s; no Aurland-specific series.
- Data end in 2016; NVE has since recalibrated again (the web page `nve2026hydrologiske` mentions adjustments from June 2026).
