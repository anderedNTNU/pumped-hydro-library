A. Case Description

   A computer model was established implementing the
HPMS algorithm described in Section II using the combined
SDP/SDDP algorithm described in Section III to solve the HPS
problem. The model was tested on a Norwegian watercourse
comprising 7 hydropower reservoirs with corresponding power
plants, with a total capacity of 986 MW. An illustration of the
topology and specification of some technical characteristics is
provided in Fig. 3. For each reservoir shown in the figure the
average annual inflow and storage capacity are stated, both in
Mm3. Each power plant is identified with a number and its
installed capacity in MW.

   A scheduling horizon of 2 years was applied with weekly
decision stages. Each week was divided into 21 time steps
allowing for energy and reserve capacity sales. Each plant is
allowed to commit a maximum of 10 % of its installed capacity
to the reserve market.
