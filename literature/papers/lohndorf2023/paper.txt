                                                                                                                             Received: May 14, 2021                       Abstract. We consider the problem of a storage owner who trades in a multisettlement
                                                                                                                             Revised: September 14, 2021; November        electricity market comprising an auction-based day-ahead market and a continuous intra-
                                                                                                                             20, 2021                                     day market. We show in a stylized model that a coordinated policy that reserves capacity
                                                                                                                             Accepted: November 25, 2021                  for the intraday market is optimal and that the gap to a sequential policy increases with
                                                                                                                             Published Online in Articles in Advance:     intraday price volatility and market liquidity. To assess the value of coordination in a real-
                                                                                                                             January 31, 2022                             istic setting, we develop a multistage stochastic program for day-ahead bidding and hourly
                                                                                                                                                                          intraday trading along with a corresponding stochastic price model. We show how tight
                                                                                                                             Area of Review: Optimization                 upper bounds can be obtained based on calculating optimal bilinear penalties for a novel
                                                                                                                                                                          information relaxation scheme. To calculate lower bounds, we propose a scenario tree gen-
                                                                                                                             https://doi.org/10.1287/opre.2021.2247       eration method that lends itself to deriving an implementable policy based on reoptimiza-
                                                                                                                                                                          tion. We use these methods to quantify the value of coordination by comparing our policy
                                                                                                                             Copyright: © 2022 INFORMS                    with a sequential policy that does not coordinate day-ahead and intraday bids. In a case
                                                                                                                                                                          study, we find that coordinated bidding is most valuable for flexible storage assets with
                                                                                                                                                                          high price impact, like pumped-hydro storage. For small assets with low price impact, like
                                                                                                                                                                          battery storage, participation in the day-ahead auction is less important and intraday trad-
                                                                                                                                                                          ing appears to be sufficient. For less flexible assets, like large hydro reservoirs without
                                                                                                                                                                          pumps, intraday trading is hardly profitable as most profit is made in the day-ahead mar-
                                                                                                                                                                          ket. A comparison of lower and upper bounds demonstrates that our policy is near-
                                                                                                                                                                          optimal for all considered assets.
