EMPS - multi area power-market simulator
EMPS (EFI's Multi-area Power-market Simulator) a software program for the optimization and simulation of
hydrothermal power systems. The model accounts for transmission constraints and hydrological differences
between subregions.
Contact person
Birger Mo
Chief Scientist
In deregulated markets, the model has become widely used for forecasting future spot prices. It is also used for
investment analyses, for example for new transmission lines or power plants. In addition to forecasts of future
spot prices, the model provides forecasts for generation, reservoir levels, water inflow, consumption, rationing,
imports/exports, and more. The model is used by most major players in the Nordic power market.
The model is continuously developed to adapt to changes in the power system and has recently been enhanced
with improved functionality for modelling short-term flexibility, including batteries, and flow-based market
coupling.
The computational methodology used in EMPS is highly efficient, enabling the model to find high-quality
solutions significantly faster than alternative solution methods.
Vansimtap and EMPS - multi area power - market simulator
These are among the most widely used energy models in the Nordic power market. They are used for
generation planning (long- and medium-term), expansion planning, and power system and market analysis. The
models are best suited for systems with a significant share of hydropower.
The models can be run as standalone applications or accessed through Python functions.
