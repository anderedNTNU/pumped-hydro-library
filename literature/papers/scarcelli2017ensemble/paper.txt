Article history:                        This paper presents a new approach for long term hydropower scheduling. In opposition to the standard
Received 14 July 2016                   Markovian stochastic dynamic programming approach, where monthly inflows are modeled according to
Received in revised form 11 April 2017  probability distribution functions, conditioned to some occurrence of inflow in the previous month, in the
Accepted 9 May 2017                     proposed approach the monthly inflows are aggregated in different time scales and then submitted to the
Available online 20 May 2017            Markovian model. The discharge decisions are then calculated by a deterministic model that optimizes
                                        the problem for one year ahead according to inflows provided by a combination of each Markovian model.
Keywords:                               Tests were conducted on hypothetical single-reservoirs hydrothermal systems using data from four real
Hydrothermal systems                    Brazilian hydro plants, with distinct hydrological regimes. The performance of the proposed method
Long term scheduling                    was evaluated through simulation, using the historical inflow data, in comparison with the standard
Stochastic dynamic programming          Markovian model. The results have shown that the proposed approach has provided spillage reduction
Aggregated inflows                      and increase on hydro productivity as well as power generation, which incurred in up to 2.1% reduction
                                        in operational costs.
