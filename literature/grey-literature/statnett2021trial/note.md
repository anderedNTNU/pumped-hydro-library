**Source file:** `source.pdf` / `source.txt` (2-page snapshot made 28.10.2026). URL: https://www.statnett.no/en/about-statnett/news-and-press-releases/news-archive-2021/trial-operation-underway-for-north-sea-link/. In English.

## Key findings
- (p. 1) NSL runs **Suldal (NO) – Blyth (UK), 720 km**, "the world's longest subsea electrical interconnector". It is built for **1,400 MW**, starts trial at **up to 700 MW**, and is phased in during a 3-month trial. Expected cost **EUR 1.6 bn**; built jointly with National Grid.
- (p. 1) **NordLink (Norway–Germany) went online "this winter"** (2020/21).
- (p. 1) Statnett: interconnectors "will probably on average contribute to a somewhat higher electricity price for Norway", improve security of supply and "in some cases curb peak prices". Norwegian prices are expected to stay below those in other European countries on average.

## Strengths
Primary source for NSL capacity, length and timing.

## Weaknesses / limitations
The snapshot does not print a publication date; a "related content" item says power transfer started on 1 October 2021. The 2021 price expectations (made before the 2022 crisis) may be outdated.
