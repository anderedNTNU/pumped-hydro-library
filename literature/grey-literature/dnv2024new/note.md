## Key findings
- (p. 1) Global electricity demand **doubles by 2050**; electricity reaches **37 % of final energy use by 2050 (20 % in 2023)**.
- (p. 1) Wind and solar produce **50 % of world electricity by 2040 and 70 % by 2050**; about 90 % non-fossil by 2050.
- (p. 1) VRES expand **ninefold** and the **need for short-term flexibility doubles**; new ancillary services (synthetic inertia, fast frequency response) are needed.
- (p. 1) Storage: **Li-ion will offer three times more storage capacity than hydropower and pumped storage by 2050** (the release does not say whether this is GW or GWh).
- (p. 2) Grid capacity must grow **2.5x**; grid spending above USD 970 bn/yr by 2050.

## Strengths
Recent, quotable headline numbers from a recognised energy consultancy.

## Weaknesses / limitations
A press release, not the report. The storage comparison metric is unclear. Global rather than Nordic, and the flexibility emphasis is short-term. Cite the full report if the numbers are used.
