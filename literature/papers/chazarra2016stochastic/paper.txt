Day-ahead electricity market           coupled hydropower plants to obtain the weekly operation that simultaneously maximizes the expected
Secondary regulation reserve market    profit in both energy and regulation reserves markets. The model is formulated for a price-taker agent, and

Cascaded reservoir system              head dependency, and fractional water travel time. The main contribution is that the obtained solution
