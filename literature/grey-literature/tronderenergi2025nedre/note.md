# Nedre Driva power plant project (TrønderEnergi, 2025)

**Source file:** `source.pdf` / `source.txt` (9-page snapshot made 28.10.2026). URL: https://www.tronderenergi.no/nedre-driva. In Norwegian. A developer's project page.

## Research question / purpose
Presents a capacity and energy expansion of Driva kraftverk (Sunndal/Oppdal) to the public, including a pump and deeper reservoir regulation.

## Setting
- **System:** Driva watercourse; reservoir Gjevilvatnet; NO3 (Mid-Norway; bidding zone inferred, not stated).
- **Markets:** not specified; mentions "rapid regulation" and system services (p. 4).

## Method
n/a. The developer analysed 25 historical years of Gjevilvatnet levels with the new regime (p. 6).

## Data / case
- **Today (p. 2):** Driva, in operation since 1973; 2 × 75 MW Francis = **150 MW**; head **565 m**; catchment 411 km²; **600 GWh/yr**. Gjevilvatnet is a drawdown reservoir without a dam, **15 m regulation** (645.8–660.8 m a.s.l.). Already has the Vassli pump (Ångårdsvatnet → Gjevilvatnet).
- **Project (pp. 1, 3–4):** new station in rock at Sunndalsøra with outlet to the fjord, **400 MW**, head +91 m (to ~660 m), **+100 GWh (+17 %)** from the extra head; 35 km tunnel, 60 m² cross-section; **Festa pump 30 m³/s** from the Driva river (spring flood) → **+400 GWh/yr**; lowest regulated level lowered **5 m ≈ 68 Mm³**; possible new brook intakes +75 GWh/yr. Total **1,100 GWh/yr, 550 MW**, i.e. +500 GWh/yr.
- **Environment (p. 5):** SINTEF feasibility study proposes minimum flows of 5 m³/s in winter (1 Oct–30 Apr) and 20 m³/s otherwise, allowing about 365 Mm³ pumped in an average year.
- **Economics (p. 6):** concession power price 2025: 12.89 øre/kWh; resource rent tax 2023 NOK 160 m, 2024 NOK 58 m (preliminary).
- **Timeline (p. 7):** notification to NVE 26 June 2025; KU programme set 3 March 2026; licence application Dec 2026; detailed design Oct 2028; **investment decision and construction start Oct 2029**.

## Key findings
- (p. 3) Motivation: NVE projections indicate that the **capacity (effekt) balance** will be challenged toward 2040; the project adds energy "but not least" capacity without large new interventions.
- (p. 4) The fjord outlet allows rapid regulation and system services without hurting salmon in the Driva river.

## Strengths
A concrete, current Norwegian example of combined capacity upgrade, pumping and deeper drawdown at an existing reservoir, with full technical numbers.

## Weaknesses / limitations
A developer's page in the early licensing phase; numbers may change. **Internal inconsistency:** p. 3 says the NVE notification is for a new **600 MW** unit (under evaluation), while pp. 1 and 4 give **400 MW** and a 550 MW total. The pump is a river intake into the reservoir (inflow augmentation), not a reversible upper/lower-reservoir PHS.
