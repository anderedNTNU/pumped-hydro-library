# One percent more output could have halved the peak price (Messel Larsen, Europower 2024)

**Source file:** `source.pdf` / `source.txt` (1 page; only headline, lede and photo caption — article body behind paywall). URL: https://www.europower.no/kraftmarked/-n-prosent-storre-produksjon-kunne-halvert-ekstremprisen/2-1-1753442. Norwegian.

## Research question / purpose
News analysis of the December 2024 price spike: how much extra capacity would have damped the peak hour.

## Setting
Southern Norway (NO2), day-ahead, single peak hour ("Thursday last week" — consistent with 12.12.2024, see andersen2024stromprisen; unverified).

## Method
Unknown (body not available); presumably a supply-curve reading of the auction.

## Key findings
- (p. 1) Price in southern Norway passed **10 NOK/kWh** on "Thursday last week".
- (p. 1) **One capacity upgrade of a hydropower plant could have halved the hourly price in the most expensive hour; 150 MW extra capacity** could have taken the edge off the highest price peak. Headline: **1 % more production**.
- (p. 1) Photo caption: energy minister at Lysebotn plant (Lyse), 2023 — context only.

## Strengths
Directly links extra MW to peak-price reduction — the same lever our capacity comparison varies.
