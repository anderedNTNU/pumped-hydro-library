## 3. Whole-thesis notes
- **How the red thread is kept between chapters:** (1) RQs are numbered "No. 1–4", repeated verbatim at the start of the results chapter, used in result-section titles and in a column of the policy tables. (2) Background chapters end with summary paragraphs that map each subsection to its later use (2.4.1, 3.7). (3) Method choices point back to literature-review subsections (correlation → joint clustering; Markov chain "identified in Section 4"). (4) Topics scoped out early promise a later section (taxes 3.2 → 10.8; activation 7.1.3 → ch. 12). (5) The intro hook (a named 90 MW upgrade) returns as the benchmark for the NPV result. (6) The convexity requirement of SDDP is the recurring reason for model choices (7.1.3, 10.8, appendix A). (7) A reuse table and a deterministic validation against the project report tie the master's thesis to its predecessor.
- **How the RQs or objectives are answered in the conclusion:** one paragraph each, in RQ order, with ordinal openers; not labelled by number; qualitative, with only one number; caveats are folded into each answer; no separate limitations or future-work pointer.
- **Anything a strong thesis does that the codes do not capture:**
  - A validation chapter that openly includes "framework challenges": infeasible years and a value-function shape that contradicts theory are shown, quantified and explained by the algorithm's mechanics.
  - Back-of-envelope calculations in background chapters to derive the thesis premise (low capacity factor of upgrades → they are bought for flexibility).
  - Reporting tried-and-rejected data-generation methods in one short paragraph each.
  - Assumption–rationale–implication table at the end of the data chapter.
  - Using the simulation stage (fixed cuts) to test a non-convex variant the SDDP training cannot handle.
  - Industry evidence (company results, real project cost) used as an external sanity check for model output.
  - Chapter order Method → Problem → Model → Data → Validation → Results, with no separate theory or discussion chapter; discussion is distributed (8.6, ch. 9, caveat paragraphs in ch. 10).
  - Weak spots worth avoiding: broken cross-references, wrong equation numbers, parameter-table labelling errors, and no runtime/convergence evidence.
