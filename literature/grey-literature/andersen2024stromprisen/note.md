# Record NO2 price spike, December 2024 (Andersen, TU 2024)

**Source file:** `source.pdf` / `source.txt` (2-page text snapshot made 28.10.2026 by the group; the snapshot says only the public text/excerpt is captured, the full article was not confirmed). Original URL: https://www.tu.no/artikler/stromprisen-hopper-opp-til-over-13-kroner-timen-inkludert-moms-i-sorvest-norge-torsdag/553823. Norwegian. Pages = snapshot pages.

## Research question / purpose
News report on next-day (Thursday 12.12.2024) day-ahead prices in southern Norway.

## Setting
- **System:** Norwegian bidding zones NO1–NO5. **Markets:** day-ahead. **Horizon:** one delivery day.

## Method
n/a (journalism; quotes Volue Insight analyst Tor Reier Lilleholt and the energy minister).

## Data / case
Day-ahead prices for 12.12.2024, figures attributed to hvakosterstrommen.no.

## Key findings
- (p. 1) NO2 maximum **10.53 NOK/kWh, 17:00–18:00**, before taxes; incl. VAT, consumption tax and Enova levy **13.39 NOK/kWh**. NO2 daily average **4.087 NOK/kWh**.
- (p. 1) Cause per Volue: **"extremely little wind in Germany" for three days**; Nordic situation itself not strained (not especially cold, good reservoir levels). Prices transmitted via the NO2–Germany link: near-equal prices for many daytime hours.
- (p. 2) Higher prices recorded in 2009/2010 (per Volue archives); in contrast to the 2022 gas crisis (persistently high), this is a spike lasting a few days.
- (p. 2) Daily averages elsewhere: NO1 2.81, NO5 1.22, NO3 0.891, **NO4 0.121 NOK/kWh** — very large zonal spread.
- (p. 2) Daily average was 1.51 NOK/kWh higher than the day before.

## Strengths
Concrete, dated numbers for an extreme scarcity event; explicit mechanism (wind lull abroad + coupling).

## Weaknesses / limitations
News article, partial snapshot; prices in NOK/kWh incl. some taxes in headline — use the ex-tax 10.53 NOK/kWh figure for wholesale. Verify against Nord Pool data before citing exact values.
