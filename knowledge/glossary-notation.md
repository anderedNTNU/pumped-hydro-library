# Glossary and notation

**The single source of truth for symbols and terms.** Check here before introducing a new symbol in the thesis or in code.
Conventions (IØT): indices and variables in lowercase, parameters in uppercase, sets in uppercase `\mathcal{}`.

## Terms (always use exactly this word)
| Term | Meaning | Don't use instead |
|---|---|---|
| long-duration energy storage (LDES) | storage that shifts energy over days to seasons; pumped storage with a seasonal reservoir is the case here | seasonal storage (ambiguous), bulk storage |
| charging capacity | the pumping power of the plant (MW), i.e. how fast the storage can be refilled | pump size, pump rating |
| adding a pump | retrofitting pumping to an existing conventional reservoir plant (RQ1) | pumped-storage conversion |
| medium-term scheduling | the level of our model: weekly stages over one year or more, water values out, hourly decisions inside the week (D-004; `helseth2016` p. 936, `gjelsvik2010` p. 35) | long-term scheduling (that is EMPS-type system models, 3–5 years), seasonal scheduling |
| long-term | only for the storage duration (LDES, the title) and for system models; never for our model's level (D-004) | — |
