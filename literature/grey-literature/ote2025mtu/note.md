# 15-minute MTU go-live in SDAC (OTE news / SDAC press release, 2025)

**Files:** `source.pdf` = the official 2-page SDAC press release dated 12 09 2025 (copied from the inbox, `%PDF` OK). `source_webpage.txt` / `source.html` = the OTE news page (the URL in the bib), saved 29.10.2026 by the group. The bib entry points to the OTE web page; the PDF is the "official project press release" linked from that page. Page references: PR p. 1–2 = press release PDF; "web" = OTE page.

## Research question / purpose
n/a. Announcement that all parties of the 15-minute MTU project in SDAC confirmed full readiness and the go-live date.

## Setting
- **System:** Single Day-Ahead Coupling (SDAC), the pan-European day-ahead market coupling (all coupled bidding zones, incl. the Nordic ones).
- **Markets:** day-ahead only.
- **Resolution:** change of the day-ahead market time unit from 60 to 15 minutes.

n/a.

## Key findings
- (PR p. 1) "Go-live is therefore scheduled for 30 September 2025 (trading day) for delivery day 1 October 2025."
- (web) The launch had originally been planned for 11 June 2025.
- (PR p. 2) SDAC couples regional wholesale markets through a common algorithm that respects cross-border constraints and maximises social welfare.

## Strengths
- Primary, official announcement of the date; short and unambiguous.

## Weaknesses / limitations
- It is an announcement **before** go-live (12 Sept 2025). It does not itself prove the go-live happened. Corroboration: Statnett's Systemutviklingsplan 2025 states that 15-minute trading in the day-ahead market "ble satt i drift 30. september 2025" (`statnett2025systemutviklingsplan`, p. 21).
- No product details (order resolution, price limits). Those are in `ph24_nordpool_specifications_2026` / `nordpool2025`.
- The bib author is "OTE" (the Czech NEMO that republished the news). The press release itself is issued jointly by the SDAC parties; OTE is just the host of the web page.
