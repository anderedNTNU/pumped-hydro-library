Procurement of automatic Frequency Restoration Reserve (aFRR) in
Q4/2026

The aFRR procurement schedule for downwards balancing will be reduced in Q4/2026 in the hours 7-19
from 325 MW to 275 MW. The volume for upwards balancing will remain unchanged.

The main basis for the procurement is the TSO goal for frequency quality level. The frequency quality has
continued to be well within the goal for 2026.

The TSOs evaluate and reassess the procurement scheme continuously.

Nordic aFRR volumes per hour

From 1st of October, the total volume for upwards balancing will vary between 200 - 250 MW and for
downwards balancing between 250 - 300 MW.
