# Potential for upgrading and extending Norwegian hydropower plants (Henriksen, Østenby & Skau / NVE, 2020)

Norwegian-language NVE fact sheet, 3 pages. Inbox file labelled "NVE_2020"; the key here follows the first named author (Better BibTeX rule). If Zotero stores it with NVE as corporate author, the key will differ.
**Page convention:** printed page = PDF page (NVE FAKTA NR. 6/2020, pp. 1–3). Read in full. Figures (Fig. 1–3) are images and not in `source.txt`; numbers below come from the text.

## Research question / purpose
Explain why estimates of the potential for upgrading ("opprusting") and extension ("utvidelse") (O/U) of Norwegian hydropower differ, how the potential can be estimated, and what may be realised in the next 20 years (p. 1).

## Setting
- **System:** the Norwegian hydropower fleet (1667 plants) (p. 1).
- **Markets:** not modelled; mentions el-certificates as a driver (p. 3).
- **Horizon and resolution:** annual energy (TWh/yr); 20-year outlook.

## Method
Qualitative/statistical overview by NVE; categorises potentials as theoretical, technical, techno-economic, economic and realistic (company-economic) (p. 2, Fig. 2).

## Data / case
NVE's resource accounts, concession records, "Samlet plan for vassdrag" and the 1986–1993 O/U mapping (p. 2, fn 2), NVE Fakta 3/2020 on turbine upgrades (p. 3, fn 3).

## Key findings
- (p. 1) Norway's theoretical hydropower potential exceeds 600 TWh; about **216 TWh** is techno-economically exploitable, of which ~**64 % is built** and ~**23 % protected**; total remaining potential ~**23 TWh**, including new projects and O/U (footnote: includes a theoretical upgrade of plants > 10 MW of 4.4 TWh).
- (p. 2, fact box) **Upgrading** = efficiency measures, reducing waterway losses, replacing components near end of life; usually within the existing concession, so no new licence. **Extension** = measures that increase head, reduce flood losses or use more water, e.g. transferring new catchments, **increasing discharge capacity ("slukeevne") or reservoir capacity**; always requires a licensing-duty assessment and possibly licensing under the watercourse legislation.
- (p. 2) About 90 % of O/U projects that applied to NVE for a licence in the last 10 years were approved. Final realisation is the companies' decision; they weigh, among other things, lost production during rebuilding and lost income during construction.
- (p. 2) NVE's O/U estimate: **7.6 TWh** (techno-economic), "a good part" of which may be realised in the next 20 years; partly from efficiency gains in necessary rehabilitations such as runner replacement.
- (p. 3) 1993 mapping with the power companies gave a techno-economic O/U potential of 16 TWh; NVE's more recent estimates are 6–8 TWh.
- (p. 3) Over the last 20 years about **half of the hydropower plants** went through reinvestment and O/U projects, adding almost **5 TWh**; el-certificates raised profitability and helped many projects in the last 8 years.
- (p. 3) Realisation depends on political framework, power price, technology and cost development, required return, climate-driven inflow changes and views on environmental impact.

## Strengths
- Authoritative Norwegian source (NVE) for the size of remaining hydro potential and the upgrade/extension distinction, including the licensing distinction.

## Weaknesses / limitations
- Energy (TWh) only: says nothing about **capacity (MW/effekt)** upgrades or pumped storage, which is what matters for flexibility and for our PHS configurations.
- Short fact sheet, no project-level data, no costs.
- Norwegian language; figures not text-extractable.
