# Flow-based market coupling in the Nordics (Shinde, Montel 2024)

**Source file:** `source.pdf` / `source.txt` (8-page snapshot made 28.10.2026; figures not reproduced, only captions). URL: https://montel.energy/commentary/flow-based-market-coupling-in-the-nordics. In English. Pages are snapshot pages.

## Research question / purpose
Explains FBMC versus ATC capacity allocation and analyses the Nordic RCC parallel-run results ahead of the Nordic FBMC go-live.

## Setting
- **System:** Nordic capacity calculation region: 12 real bidding zones plus 19 virtual HVDC zones, 31 in all (p. 4).
- **Markets:** day-ahead (SDAC/Euphemia); qualitative remarks on intraday and balancing.
- **Horizon:** parallel-run data from 12 Dec 2022 to 30 Jun 2024.

## Method
Descriptive data analysis of JAO-published parallel-run results (flows, net positions, price differences, daily spreads, price correlations), comparing ATC and FBMC.

## Data / case
Nordic RCC parallel runs (JAO); FB domain examples SE3/NO5 (14 Sep and 14 Nov 2023, 20–21 CET).

## Key findings
- (p. 1) CWE FBMC started May 2015; **Core DA FBMC launched 8 June 2022** (13 member states, 12 bidding zones).
- (p. 2, p. 7) Nordic FBMC planned go-live **October 2024**.
- (p. 2–3) ATC vs FBMC definitions; PTDF (sensitivity of a CNEC to a zone's net position), GSK, CNEC; capacity calculation then allocation.
- (p. 3) Euphemia heuristic time limit **17 min, to be extended to 30 min for 15-min MTU**.
- (p. 4) **RAM = Fmax − FRM − F0 − AAC + RA + AMR − IVA**; AAC is used to represent FRR (reserve) allocations on CNECs.
- (p. 5) FBMC changes net positions most in Norway; NO2 affected by larger NO1→NO2 flows. "Producers in NO2 would also need to adjust their water value."
- (p. 5) **Price differences between zones fall under FBMC on most borders** (more price convergence).
- (p. 6) **Average daily max–min spreads fall under FBMC in most zones and seasons**, which indicates lower volatility. Negative-price peaks are less extreme (NO3), but SE3 winter morning and evening peaks are higher.
- (p. 6) Price correlations rise in most but not all zone pairs (for example NO3 with SE1, SE2 and NO4 falls).
- (p. 7) Caveats: counterintuitive flows; **hydro water values change with the new capacities, so the parallel-run prices (computed with ATC-era bids) "do not entirely reflect the reality"**; concerns about reduced intraday capacity (interim ATCE method); impact on single imbalance pricing still open.

## Strengths
Clear technical explanation plus quantitative parallel-run comparison specific to the Nordics, including NO zones.

## Weaknesses / limitations
An analyst blog, not peer reviewed; figures and tables are not in the text snapshot, so the numbers in Table 1 and Figs. 7–11 cannot be read from our copy. Written before go-live: Nordic FBMC actually went live on 29 October 2024 (from general knowledge; unverified here), so post-go-live outcomes are not covered. Parallel runs ignore changes in bidding behaviour.
